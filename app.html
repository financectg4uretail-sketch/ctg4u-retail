Xero posting

Agent / pass-through model. Pharmacy collections are not CTG4U revenue — they are a liability owed to the brand owner (account 320). Only the management fee (500) and service fee (510) hit the P&L.
Check before the first import: *TaxType values must match a tax rate that actually exists in CTG4U RETAIL's Xero, and the account codes must exist in its chart of accounts. Xero rejects the whole CSV row otherwise.

Xero tracking

Tags every invoice line with a Xero tracking option, so a brand owner’s activity reports separately inside Xero without a separate account for each. Leave the category blank to switch it off. Both the category and the option must exist in Xero exactly as spelled — the connector checks every option it is about to use and refuses the run rather than losing the tag on one line.
